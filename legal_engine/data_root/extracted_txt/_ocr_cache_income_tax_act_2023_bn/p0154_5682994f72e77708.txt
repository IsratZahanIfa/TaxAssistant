 করিবার কারণ থাকে যে, উক্ত ব্যক্তি তাহার হিসাব, যদি, ইস্পেশাল অডিট রিপোর্ট এবং টিপসসমূহ submits করেন অথবা মূল্যবান ধাতু precious metal কোনো মূল্যবান সামগ্রী submit করিয়াছেন;

(ঘ) আর সম্পর্কিত রেকর্ড, তথ্য, মূল্যবান ধাতু precious metal বা অন্যান্য মূল্যবান সামগ্রী বা হিসাবটি verify করিতে assist করেন persons প্রত্যেক ব্যক্তি inspection-এর জন্য প্রস্তুত থাকিবেন;

(ঙ) আর সম্পর্কিত রেকর্ড ও documents furnish না করিলে বা ইচ্ছাকৃতভাবে withhold করেন। এ statement তৈরি বা তৈরি সহায়তা করিয়া কোনো ব্যক্তি false statement দাখিল করিলে বা তৈরি সহায়তা withholding বা তৈরি করিয়া false statement প্রস্তুত করেন;

(চ) আর মূল্যবান ধাতু precious metal বা অন্যান্য মূল্যবান সামগ্রী বা documents মোট তিনি বা তিনি inspection-এর জন্য প্রস্তুত থাকিবেন এবং

(ছ) ইস্পেশাল রিপোর্ট এবং টিপসসমূহ submit জন্য, যদি কোনো person ইচ্ছাকৃতভাবে documents অথবা statements withhold করিয়া inspection-এ assist করেন। এ ব্যক্তি false statement তৈরি করিয়া তথ্য withholding বা তৈরি সহায়তা করিয়া false statement প্রস্তুত করেন।

(১) Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-section (1), the Central Government or the State Government may, by notification, direct that any person possessing information relating to any contravention of this Act shall furnish such information in such form and manner as may be specified therein; and if any person fails to comply with such direction, he shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to one year or with fine or with both.

(2) Without prejudice to the provisions of section 159, if any person possesses any documents, records, information or other things relating to any contravention of this Act, the Central Government or the State Government may, by warrant, require such person to produce such documents, records, information or other things for inspection; and if such person fails to comply with the said warrant, he shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to one year or with fine or with both.

(৩) Where any person is required under this Act to submit books, accounts, documents or other records, he shall also be deemed to have submitted copies thereof; and such copies shall be treated as original documents for the purposes of this Act.

(৪) The Central Government may, by notification, specify the form in which books, accounts, documents and other records are to be kept by any class of persons or institutions referred to in section 162.

(৫) Nothing contained in sub-section (1) of section 209 shall apply to any person who is required to submit statements under this Act.

(৬) The Central Government may, by rules, regulate the procedure for carrying out inspection and audit under this Act.

(৭) Where any person fails to comply with any direction issued under sub-section (1) of section 160 or with any warrant issued under sub-section (2) of section 161, the Central Government or the State Government may, after due inquiry, seize and confiscate such documents, records, information or other things.

(৮) No suit shall lie in any civil court to challenge the validity of any direction issued under sub-section (1) of section 160 or with any warrant issued under sub-section (2) of section 161; and all suits or proceedings challenging such direction or warrant shall be deemed to be filed in violation of this Act.

(৯) The Central Government may, by notification, constitute an Appellate Authority to hear and dispose of appeals against orders passed by any authority subordinate thereto under this Act.

(১০) The Appellate Authority constituted under sub-section (9) shall have the same powers as vested in a civil court for the purposes of examining evidence, recording depositions and passing such other proceedings as it deems fit; and its decision shall be final.
